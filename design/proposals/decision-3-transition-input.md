# Decision 3 — Coordinator input on the signature transition

`design/proposals/decision-3-transition-input.md` · 2026-09-16 · Input requested by the Owner when transcribing decision 3. The locked text in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §3 stands as written; every item below is optional and enters the lock only by an AUTH (design-change). Reply with item numbers.

## What the locked text already gets right

A 2.5 s hero with its own budget (rule 7); a cached low-resolution blurred backdrop instead of a live blur (rule 27, and it hides splat LOD pops during the camera move); Reduce Motion keeps the beam ignition (rule 8); tap-to-skip on repeats (rule 9).

## Visual improvement

1. **Scale anchor first (0.3 s, inside the 2.5 s).** Hold the avatar at real size in the real room with its contact shadow already visible before the pull-back begins, so the shrink reads as *you* shrinking (rule 23: the transition must make the scale relationship obvious).
2. **Summit revealed last, with a look.** Ignite the beam as the final beat and let the camera nudge 5 to 10 degrees toward it before settling into play framing, so the first goal is legible before control returns (rule 22, the introduce beat; Bible §7 look-at beam).
3. **Sound and haptics.** One rising tone across the pull-back that resolves on ignition; the Tier 0 dust-puff sound on the drop; haptics light on the drop and medium on ignition (Bible §9). Silence is the largest gap in the current text.
4. **Soften the rim, not the room.** Do the edge softening as depth-of-field on the diorama's rim rather than a uniform blur, so the center stays photoreal (rule 23: never obscure the room with effects).
5. **Amber discipline.** The beam core and the dust puff are the only amber in the frame (decision 2: amber under 10 % of any screen); any diorama edge glow stays neutral.

## Retention

6. **Short form on return, full form on milestones.** Repeats play a 0.8 s cut that keeps the drop and the ignition; the full 2.5 s returns for first entry, first summit, and after a flag plant. The moment stays special without taxing the fifth session (rules 7 and 9).
7. **Reverse transition as the results backdrop.** After a flag plant, the diorama grows back into the room behind the results screen with the flag visible; that frame is the share image (Peak-End; rule 28: the diorama is the hero shot). This is the clip people post.
8. **Make the wait part of the story.** While the splat streams, show the cached blurred room resolving into the sharp room, then the shrink (rule 11: never a blank spinner; waits over 10 s need something to look at). The player never sees a spinner between "Generate" and play.
9. **Skip never loses orientation.** A skip still plays the 0.3 s beam ignition (Bible §8).
10. **iPhone Duo unfold** (ticket M3-DUO-02): the unfold triggers the same sequence; the scale-anchor beat lives on the outer display before the unfold.

## Not recommended

A longer transition, particles beyond the dust puff, or a title card. Each delays the first playable moment (rule 9) and competes with the room (rule 1).
