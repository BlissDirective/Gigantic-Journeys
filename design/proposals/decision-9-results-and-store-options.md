# Decision 9 — Results and store screens: options for the Owner's lock

`design/proposals/decision-9-results-and-store-options.md` · 2026-09-16 · Coordinator. Binding constraints (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §9; Design Skills rules 4, 16, 26, 28; SPEC §3.8; decision 3 as amended by AUTH #004: the reverse transition is the results backdrop): one primary action, Share; the store after a win, never on a loss; two SKUs with clear prices; one-tap restore; no fake scarcity; the results screen carries the detail the HUD omits. Reply `APPROVED: decision 9 = recommended package`, or list the letters that differ; the Coordinator assigns the AUTH number when logging.

## 9a. Results layout

- **A (recommended). The flag frame.** Backdrop: the reverse transition ending on the diorama with the flag visible. Centered card on a scrim: summit time, each route's time (this run and best), vistas found as three sparkles, and a compact row of the moves used with counts (mantle, climb, jump, hang). Primary action Share; secondary Journey again and Next route. After the first summit of an environment the rating sheet (decision 8c) slides up once Share has been offered.
- B. A stacked stats list with Share at the bottom. Reads like a spreadsheet.

## 9b. Share format

- **A (recommended). Six seconds, no UI.** A vertical clip: 2 s of the reverse transition, then 4 s of the best moment, auto-picked (the flag plant, or the longest jump if the run was a route). The wordmark sits small at the bottom right; no HUD, no controls. A still of the flag frame ships alongside for platforms that want an image.
- B. Still only. Cheaper, and clips are what travel.

## 9c. Store placement

- **A (recommended). After a summit, below the fold.** On the results screen, after a summit only, a card "Dress your avatar" (outfit pack) and "Realism+" (materials) with prices inline, each previewed on the player's own avatar standing in the diorama. Restore purchases is one tap on the store sheet and in Settings. The card never appears after a fall or a quit, never before the first summit of the account, and never covers Share (rules 16 and 26).
- B. A store tab in the shell. Always available, always ignored, and it invites the "shop first" pattern the rules forbid.

## 9d. Store copy and rules

- **A (recommended).** Verbs first: "Dress your avatar", "Sharpen the look". Prices in local currency from StoreKit. No timers, badges, countdowns, or "limited" language. Purchases apply immediately in the diorama preview. Family Sharing and restore work on day one.

## 9e. Failure and loss screens

- **A (recommended).** A fall or a quit shows the diorama with the avatar at the last stable surface and one primary action, Keep going; the store, ratings, and share are absent. Time penalties are shown as a small "+0:04" next to the timer, never as a red banner (SPEC §3.5: falls cost time only).

## Recommended package

9a-A, 9b-A, 9c-A, 9d-A, 9e-A.
