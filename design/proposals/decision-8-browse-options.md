# Decision 8 — Browse and ranking cards: options for the Owner's lock

`design/proposals/decision-8-browse-options.md` · 2026-09-16 · Coordinator. Binding constraints (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §8; Design Skills rules 24, 25; SPEC §3.7; decision 2 vocabulary): browse by place, thumbnails are the room; sort tabs Top this week, New, Near your scale; one-tap four-axis rating; one-tap report with a reason sheet; creator stats visible; "Under review" badge. Reply `APPROVED: decision 8 = recommended package`, or list the letters that differ; the Coordinator assigns the AUTH number when logging.

## 8a. The card

- **A (recommended). The diorama is the card.** A full-bleed diorama thumbnail (the environment as a tiny world, the hero shot of rule 28) on a 4:5 card in a two-column grid. A bottom scrim carries the creator's name, the number of summits reached, and the four-axis score as four short bars (fun, interesting, interactive, exciting). No title is required; the place is the title.
- B. One-column list with a wide thumbnail and a text block. More words, fewer places per screen.

## 8b. Sort tabs

- **A (recommended).** Top this week · New · Near your scale (a Room / Tabletop segment inside the tab). Friends arrives with social features later.
- B. A single algorithmic feed. Opaque; creators cannot see how to be found.

## 8c. Rating

- **A (recommended). Four taps, three seconds.** After a summit, or on leaving an environment, one sheet with four rows (fun, interesting, interactive, exciting), each a five-segment bar rated with a tap; Skip is always visible; the sheet never blocks the results screen's Share action.
- B. Thumbs up or down. Faster, but it starves the ranking model of the four signals SPEC §3.7 needs.

## 8d. Report

- **A (recommended).** A flag icon on the card back and in the pause sheet; one tap opens the reason sheet: private information visible · inappropriate content · not a real place · broken environment · other. Sending shows "Under review" immediately on the reporter's view; the creator sees the badge until the queue clears it (rule 25).

## 8e. Creator stats

- **A (recommended).** On your own environments: plays, summits, best time per route, ratings, all on the card back (tap to flip) and on a profile page. Stats are visible to the creator only in v1; public creator pages come with social features.

## 8f. Deep links

- **A (recommended).** An environment link opens its card with one primary action, Journey; if the app is not installed, the App Store page with the diorama thumbnail. The link carries no location data (SECURITY_CHECKLIST §4.4).

## 8g. Moderation visibility

- **A (recommended).** Environments awaiting the vision pass show a small "Under review" badge and are playable only by their creator until cleared; cleared environments show nothing. A rejected environment tells the creator why, in one line, with an appeal action.

## Recommended package

8a-A, 8b-A, 8c-A, 8d-A, 8e-A, 8f-A, 8g-A.
