How we handle your captures
When you capture a room or a tabletop, the video goes to our servers so we can rebuild it in 3D. We strip location data on your phone and again on our servers. We keep the video only until your environment is ready, then delete it. You can delete everything from Settings at any time, and we finish within 30 days.
