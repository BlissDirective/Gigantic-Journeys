## 5. Moderation, reporting, blocking, and enforcement (UGC — Apple Guideline 1.2)
We operate the four controls Apple requires for user-generated content, and commit to enforcing them:
- **(1) Filter objectionable content.** Every environment passes an **automated content-moderation pass before it becomes public**; a submission the filter clears goes live, and one it flags is held "Under review" and routed to human review (the *auto-clear + human-on-report* model, SPEC §3.7, `SECURITY_CHECKLIST §10.5`).
- **(2) Report + timely response.** Every published environment and every user can be reported in-app with one tap and a reason. **We act on reports within 24 hours** — removing objectionable content and ejecting the offending user as warranted. A reported item shows an "Under review" state to the reporter immediately.
- **(3) Block abusive users.** You can block a user so you no longer see their content, and they can no longer interact with yours.
- **(4) Published contact information.** Our developer contact information is published in-app and on our website and App Store product page (see §11) so you can reach us about content and conduct.
- **Enforcement.** We may remove content, revoke publishing, and suspend or terminate accounts that violate these rules, with escalation to the publisher and, for illegal content, to the appropriate authorities.
